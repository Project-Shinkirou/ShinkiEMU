# Project-Shinkirou Brand Kit / 品牌规范

This directory contains the canonical visual identity assets for **Project-Shinkirou** and **ShinkiEMU**.

本目录保存 **Project-Shinkirou** 与 **ShinkiEMU** 的标准视觉识别资产。

## Visual Theme: Clear Sky Mirage（晴空蜃景）

The brand draws from the image of a mirage pavilion (*Shinkirou*, 蜃気楼) reflected across a clear horizon — pairing a luminous Japanese visual-novel sky palette with minimalist geometric precision.

品牌意象源自晴空水面上倒映的「蜃楼（Shinkirou）」，将清透明亮的日系视觉小说晴空色调与现代极简几何线条结合。

## Naming & Usage Guidelines / 命名与使用规范

- **Spelling**: Write **Project-Shinkirou**, **ShinkiEMU**, and **RoukakuEngine** with their exact capitalization.
- **Clear space**: Keep at least `16%` optical padding around the geometric mark when placing it inside badges, launchers, or headers. Do not stretch, skew, outline heavily, or alter the horizon alignment.
- **Free & open-source integrity**: ShinkiEMU and RoukakuEngine are free, open-source software. Do not use the Project-Shinkirou or ShinkiEMU name, icon, or brand assets to imply official endorsement of paid redistributions, repackaged commercial games, or unauthorized bundles.
