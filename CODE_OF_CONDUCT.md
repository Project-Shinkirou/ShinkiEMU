# Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

[English](#english) · [简体中文](#简体中文)

---

<a id="english"></a>

## English

Project-Shinkirou is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and productive community for everyone — players, testers, translators, patch authors, and developers alike.

### Expected Behavior

- Communicate constructively, patiently, and with empathy across languages and experience levels.
- Credit upstream authors, translators, and contributors accurately.
- Keep technical feedback focused on code, facts, and reproducible observations.
- Respect copyright and legal boundaries: never share commercial game data, pirated copies, decryption keys, or copyrighted scripts/assets.

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, hate speech, or discriminatory remarks.
- Sharing pirated games, commercial assets, private credentials, or unredacted personal data.
- Promoting paid redistributions or scams impersonating Project-Shinkirou or ShinkiEMU.

### Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or close comments, issues, discussions, or pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct, and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations. Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).

## 简体中文

Project-Shinkirou 致力于为玩家、测试者、译者、补丁作者与开发者营造友善、尊重、务实的开源社区环境。

### 我们倡导

- 跨语言、跨经验层次保持耐心、友善与建设性的沟通；
- 尊重并准确署名上游引擎作者、译者与社区贡献者；
- 讨论问题聚焦于可复现的事实、日志与技术方案；
- 遵守版权与合法边界：仅使用合法取得的游戏，不分享商业游戏文件、盗版资源、密钥或受版权保护的正文与素材。

### 不可接受的行为

- 人身攻击、骚扰、歧视性或仇恨言论；
- 传播盗版游戏、商业素材、他人凭据或未脱敏的隐私数据；
- 推广付费倒卖或冒充 Project-Shinkirou / ShinkiEMU 官方的诈骗行为。

### 执行说明

维护者有权编辑、隐藏或关闭违反本准则的评论、Issue、讨论或 PR，并对严重或屡次违规者限制社区参与权限。本准则改编自 [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-cn/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)。
